## WHAT IS CIVICAI?

CivicAI is a website and app that helps Indian citizens find and apply for government schemes — like scholarships, farmer support, health insurance, housing, and pensions.

Think of it like a very smart assistant that:
- Speaks your language (13 Indian languages)
- Listens to your voice (you can speak instead of typing)
- Knows about your family
- Tells you exactly which government programs you and your family qualify for
- Explains why you qualify — in simple words
- Links you directly to the official government website to apply

---

## THE PROBLEM WE'RE SOLVING

### Many Indians miss out on government benefits

Here's why:

| Problem | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **People don't know schemes exist** | The government has hundreds of schemes, but most people have never heard of them |
| **Too many websites** | Each scheme is on a different website — it's confusing |
| **Rules are complicated** | "Am I eligible? What documents do I need? How do I apply?" — hard to figure out |
| **Language barrier** | Most websites are in English or Hindi only — what if you speak Tamil or Bengali? |
| **Can't read or write?** | If you can't read, you can't use most government websites |
| **No family view** | No website shows what your whole family qualifies for — only individual views |

### The Result

Millions of Indians miss out on money and benefits they're entitled to — simply because they don't know about them or can't access them.

CivicAI doesn't replace government websites. It links you to them.

**Status: Implemented** — This is working today. The links go to real government websites.

CivicAI gives every recommendation a confidence score (0-100%):

- **90-100%**: Very confident — you almost certainly qualify

If CivicAI is not sure, it tells you. It doesn't pretend to know everything.

## HOW CIVICAI MAKES SURE IT'S SAFE

### The AI Can't Make Wrong Decisions

Here's the most important thing to understand about CivicAI:

> **The AI explains. It does NOT decide.**

There are two parts:

1. **The Rule Engine** — This is a set of fixed rules that decides if you qualify. It's like a checklist. If your income is below ₹2 lakh and you're a student, you qualify for the scholarship. The rule engine checks this. It's 100% accurate — no guessing.

2. **The AI (LLM)** — This is the part that talks to you. It takes the rule engine's decision and explains it in your language. It says "You qualify because you're a student with income below ₹2 lakh."

**Why this matters:** The AI cannot accidentally tell you that you qualify when you don't. The rule engine decides. The AI only explains.

**Status: Implemented** — This is working today.

### Your Data Is Protected

|---|---|---|

| Data minimization | Only necessary data is collected | Prototype / Simulated |

### You Can Always Verify

CivicAI always tells you:
- The official government website (so you can verify)
- The policy version (so you know which rules apply)
- The confidence score (so you know how sure the AI is)
- That you should always check with official sources

**CivicAI does not replace government websites. It helps you find and use them.**

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Q: Is CivicAI free to use?
**A:** The prototype is free. In production, the goal is to keep CivicAI free for citizens.

### Q: Do I need an Aadhaar card to use CivicAI?
**A:** No. In the current prototype, Aadhaar is simulated. You can use CivicAI with just an email address or Google account.

### Q: Can CivicAI apply for schemes for me?
**A:** No. CivicAI links you to the official government website where you apply yourself. In the future, CivicAI may support direct application submission (Future Integration).

### Q: Does CivicAI store my data?
**A:** CivicAI stores your family member data (name, age, income, etc.) in a secure database. Your data is private — only you can see it. In the future, consent management will give you full control over who accesses your data (Prototype / Simulated).

### Q: Can I trust the AI's recommendations?
**A:** The AI's eligibility recommendations are based on a deterministic rule engine (not AI guessing). However, you should always verify with the official government website. CivicAI provides the direct link for verification.

### Q: What if the AI is wrong?
**A:** The rule engine makes eligibility decisions — it's a fixed checklist, not AI guessing. If the AI's explanation is inaccurate, the rule engine's decision is still correct. You can always verify with the official portal. For high-stakes cases, a human officer reviews (Prototype / Simulated).

### Q: Does CivicAI work in my language?
**A:** CivicAI supports 13 Indian languages. 8 are fully translated (Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, English). 5 are partially translated (Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, Odia, Assamese).

### Q: Can I use CivicAI if I can't read?
**A:** Yes. You can press the microphone button and speak in your language. The AI will understand and respond. You can press "Read Aloud" to hear the answer.

### Q: Is CivicAI connected to the government?
**A:** No. CivicAI is a private prototype. It is not connected to any government database, API, or service. It links to official government websites but doesn't integrate with them. Government partnership is needed for production (Future Integration).

### Q: Is my data safe?
**A:** CivicAI uses Base44-managed security (encryption, authentication, user-scoped data). Your data is encrypted and only accessible to you. Advanced security controls (rate limiting, audit logging, consent enforcement) are planned for future phases (Future Integration).
